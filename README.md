# Monitoring and Evaluation


Portfolio of the assigments from the ITCILO M&amp;E Certification Programme. 

All the assignments have been completed referring to data from real-world case studies. 

## Content of the course:

## Module 1 – Introduction to Monitoring and Evaluation

* Aid effectiveness agenda
* Results-Based Management (RBM) overview
* Results chain, logical framework, theory of change
* Relationship between policies, programmes and projects
* Types of monitoring and evaluation
* M&E as part of oversight functions and evidence-based decision-making

---

## Module 2 – Designing and Planning of M&E Systems

* Six-step M&E system design process
* Purpose, scope and users of M&E systems
* Stakeholder information needs
* Logic models and evaluability
* Indicators and performance measurement design
* Planning M&E activities, processes and constraints

---

## Module 3 – Data Collection and Management

* Data collection methods (surveys, interviews, observation, desk review)
* Quantitative and qualitative data
* Triangulation and sampling
* Indicator monitoring matrices
* Data management systems and planning
* Risk management and risk registers

---

## Module 4 – Data Processing, Analysis and Interpretation

* Data analysis process (preparation, analysis, presentation, verification, action planning)
* Quantitative and qualitative analysis techniques
* Diagnostic analysis tools
* Bias in data collection and interpretation
* Transforming data into findings and recommendations
* Data validation and interpretation processes

---

## Module 5 – Reporting, Communication and Use of Findings

* Reporting strategies and planning
* Types of reports and reporting formats
* Audience and communication needs
* Dissemination channels and timing
* Use of findings in decision-making
* Learning and accountability through reporting

---

## Module 6 – Capacity Building and Strengthening

* Capacity development approaches (beyond training)
* Three spheres of capacity development
* Core capacity issues
* Organisational readiness for M&E
* Capacity needs at project level
* Terms of reference and evaluator recruitment profiles

---

## Module 7 – M&E Budget, Procurement and Ethics

* Role of the evaluation manager
* Terms of Reference design
* Evaluation budgeting
* Recruitment, procurement and contracting of evaluators
* Ethical principles in evaluation
* Ethical risks and safeguards in evaluation management

---

## Module 8 – M&E theory and practice

* Evaluation paradigms and methodological choices
* Evaluation approaches, methods and techniques
* Evaluation matrix design and use
* Data collection, analysis and triangulation in evaluations
* Findings, conclusions, recommendations and follow-up
* Real-world constraints in evaluation practice
* Evaluation use, credibility and professional practice
